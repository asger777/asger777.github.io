Lineburst Privacy Policy

Effective 10 August 2026

No accounts, no sign-in, no tracking. Your game stays on your device. The only third party is Google AdMob, serving one optional, non-personalized ad type.

What Lineburst stores

Your best score, run history and stats, your in-progress game, and your settings (sound, haptics, color-blind mode) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own container. They are never uploaded anywhere — Lineburst has no server and no account system. Deleting the app deletes this data with it.

Advertising

Lineburst shows exactly one kind of ad: an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to continue a run after game over. There are no banner ads and no forced ads between games. If you purchase Premium, the app never requests an ad at all.

Ads are served by Google AdMob configured for non-personalized ads only. The app does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not track you across apps or websites. To serve and secure ads, Google may process technical data such as your IP address (which implies a coarse location), a device identifier, ad interaction data, and diagnostics. This data is not linked to your identity by the app and is not used by the app to track you. How Google handles it: Google Privacy Policy · AdMob & data.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, a consent dialog may appear before ads are served, as required by law.

Purchases

The optional Premium purchase is processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. Restore any time from Settings → Restore Purchases.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores are submitted to the Lineburst leaderboards through Apple's service under Apple's privacy terms. Not signed in? No scores go anywhere.

Children

Lineburst is a general-audience app: no chat, no user-generated content, no personalized advertising.
