Common questions

How does scoring work?

Every placed cell scores a point. Completing a row or column clears it — one line is good, several at once is much better, and clearing on back-to-back placements builds a combo streak that multiplies the points. Empty the whole board for a big all-clear bonus.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Everyone in the world gets the same sequence of pieces each day (the day flips at 00:00 UTC). Your first attempt of the day is ranked on the Daily leaderboard; after that you can practice the same puzzle as often as you like, unranked.

How does “continue” work?

Once per run, after game over, you can watch a short ad to clear four random rows and keep playing. With Premium the continue is instant — no ad. It's the only ad in the game; there are no banners and no forced ads.

I bought Premium — how do I restore it on a new phone?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

The “Watch Ad to Continue” button sometimes isn’t there

The continue is offered once per run, and the button only appears when an ad is actually loaded — with no connection (or no ad available) it hides rather than making you wait. Premium removes the ad requirement entirely.

What does color-blind mode change?

Settings → Color-blind mode switches to a color-blind-safe palette and adds a distinct shape to every block color, so no information rides on color alone.

My scores aren't on the leaderboard

Leaderboards need Game Center: sign in via iOS Settings → Game Center, then finish a run. Classic runs submit automatically; daily runs submit only the day's first (ranked) attempt.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device — see the privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes your scores, history, and any run in progress.
