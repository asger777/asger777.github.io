Streakflame Privacy Policy

Effective 7 August 2026

Your data never leaves your device. Streakflame collects nothing. Full stop.

What Streakflame stores

Your habits and check-ins are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own container. They are never uploaded anywhere — Streakflame has no server, no account system, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

What Streakflame sends

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Notifications

Reminders and streak alerts are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification system. They are created and delivered entirely on-device.

Widgets & Live Activities

Widgets and the lock-screen countdown read the same on-device data as the app, through Apple's App Group mechanism. Nothing is shared with any third party.

If you delete the app

Deleting Streakflame deletes your data with it. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing else to delete — and nothing we could recover.
