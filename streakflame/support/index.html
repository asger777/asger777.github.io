Common questions

I checked in but the widget still shows yesterday's state

Widgets refresh on their own schedule. Opening the app or tapping the widget's check-in button updates it immediately; otherwise it refreshes within a few minutes and always at midnight.

I forgot to log yesterday — is my streak gone?

Long-press the check-in button on a habit's detail screen and choose "I did it yesterday". Only yesterday can be fixed — honest streaks.

What's the countdown on my lock screen?

That's deadline mode: when you leave the app with a habit unchecked, a gentle countdown to midnight appears. You can turn it off in Settings → Deadline mode.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. No account, no cloud, no analytics — see the privacy policy. Note this also means deleting the app deletes your habit history.

How do I restore Streakflame Pro on a new phone?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.
